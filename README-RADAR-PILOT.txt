ONIT v0.22.1 — RADAR ROLE ALIGNMENT PRIVATE PILOT

WHAT CHANGED
- Creative Leadership (DCD/CD/MD) now receives agency-wide operational Radar attention.
- System Owner Radar is now reserved for platform/admin attention: pending signups, incomplete staff setup and organisation/access configuration.
- Traffic, Account Management, Team Lead and Staff Radar remain role-specific.
- Duplicate brief cards are suppressed so one job cannot consume multiple Radar slots.
- No employee productivity scoring and no automatic decisions.

DEPLOYMENT
Private pilot only. Upload index.html, service-worker.js and version.json to cases-private-pilot and test both a Leadership account and the System Owner account before live rollout.
